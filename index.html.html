Background

A strategic communicator
built on broadcast precision

Leah R.H. Robinson, APR is the founder and principal of MLR LLC | dba MLR Public Relations, a boutique public relations firm delivering executive-level communications strategy with the rigor of a broadcast newsroom.

Concurrently, she is Account Manager for Blend Marketing, where she serves as the primary point of contact between clients and the delivery team, from day-to-day contact through to C-Suite stakeholders.

Over two decades, she has driven brand visibility and thought leadership for organizations across B2B/SaaS, enterprise technology, logistics and supply chain, and the nonprofit sector — building media relationships and securing coverage that moves business outcomes, not just impressions.

Her integrated approach — spanning media relations, analyst relations, crisis communications, and content strategy — positions clients at the intersection of credibility and relevance. She is a member of the Public Relations Society of America (PRSA) and holds the APR (Accredited in Public Relations) credential, the profession's gold standard.
